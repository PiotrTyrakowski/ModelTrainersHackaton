# Second-paper test — 26 September 2026

The frozen tiny-model harness scored **9/60 on the 2024 paper**, compared with
**8/60 without retrieval**. Both full runs processed all 40 questions, with two
truncated responses each and no grades pending. These are provisional local CKE
rubric scores, not official competition results.

| Paper | System | Points / 60 | Valid answers | Model calls | Question time |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 development | v6 retrieval + prepared essay | 24 | 35/37 | 36 | 115.82 s |
| 2024 development | Same answer contract, no retrieval | 8 | 38/40 | 40 | 127.56 s |
| 2024 development | Frozen v6 retrieval + essay route | 9 | 38/40 | 40 | 145.38 s |

The 2023–2024 difference is not a paired improvement/regression estimate: the
papers contain different questions and differ in difficulty and input preparation.
The direct-versus-retrieval comparison on 2024 is paired. No answers were selected
from different runs to construct an artificial best-of-both score.

## What transferred, and what did not

The complete answer schema worked for all five closed tasks in both 2024 runs.
Both earned the same 4/7 closed-task points, again choosing F, P, F on both
true/false tasks. Closed formatting is reliable here, but that repeated pattern
does not demonstrate historical reasoning.

The essay route correctly rejected all three 2024 topics. It has no prepared essay
for Charlemagne, seventeenth-century Ottoman wars or Pilsudski. The direct model
then returned 144 words discussing the instructions rather than writing a
historical essay: 0/15. With retrieval, the model hit the output cap and returned
invalid JSON: 0/15. That failed row has no returned routing trace, so its no-match
decision was separately reproduced offline with the pinned inputs. The successful
direct row contains the actual no-match trace.

Retrieval gained one point on 12.1 (Jan II Kazimierz) and one on 22.2 (the second
source-comparison similarity), but lost the point earned by direct on 21 (the
propaganda photograph). Net change: +1, with important grading uncertainty. It
also introduced unrelated material: for example, the NSDAP image answer referred
to the Gniezno Congress and Katyn. Several image interpretations are invented in
both systems. Source relevance and visual reading remain major weaknesses.

The results do **not** support a general passing claim. The 2023 essay gain came
from a prepared entry chosen for an already inspected topic. More work should
address retrieval rejection, source interpretation and factual reasoning, then be
checked on both fixed papers. Do not write essays for every evaluation topic and
call the resulting scores evidence of unseen-question performance.

## Grading and uncertainty

The five closed questions were scored automatically from the separate CKE key.
Codex reviewed 33 successful open answers per variant against separate official
rubrics; two technical failures per variant earned zero. The rubric-only text was
checked against the complete extracted marking guide: differences consist of
removed page furniture and, for the essay, general curriculum requirements.
No marking examples or correct choices entered the solver input or corpus.

Positive awards worth independent review include 8.2 and 15.1 in both systems:
the required core appears alongside false extra claims. Removing both gives
6/60 direct and 7/60 retrieval. The retrieval award of two points on 22.2 is also
a judgment call because the correct similarities accompany garbled and false
digressions; removing that award as well gives 5/60 retrieval. Conversely, an
examiner might accept direct's incomplete Augustus identification or its second
similarity on 22.2. These examples are sensitivity checks, not confidence intervals
or exhaustive bounds. The observed one-point advantage is too weak to settle
whether retrieval helps reliably.

## Data, runtime and verification

The [2024 review](../exams/history-2024-review.md) corrected extraction boundaries,
preserved all 40 tasks and source texts, and checked 20 original figure crops used
by 30 image-bearing items. The paper had already received a topic audit; it is a
second development test, not an untouched holdout.

Model weights, v6 solver code, corpus and essay bank were held fixed before the
run. It used local Apple M5 Metal, Ollama 0.34.4 and Python 3.12.14. Learned model
files still total 1,036,034,688 bytes; no new neural encoder, fine-tuning, paid
service or internet access during inference was used. The configured cap remained
one call, 120 seconds and 2,200 output tokens per question, with 8,192 context tokens.

Direct failures: 16.1 and 25. Retrieval failures: 16.1 and 26. Answer exports
validate all 40 IDs, including two blanks per system for those failures. All
35 checkpoint tests and the existing 30 package tests pass; the new data builder
compiles and its real output passes the full coverage preflight. Runtime model
digest and Metal loading were verified and no input-prompt truncation warnings
were found. The local model server was stopped after the runs.

Full original responses, separate grading records, model identity evidence and
answer exports are retained in `artifacts/checkpoints/qwen35-2024-transfer-v1/`.
No competition submission was made.
